# Capstone

The capstone challenge brings together **all five AI-901 domains** into a single end-to-end scenario.

## What you'll demonstrate

- Map business requirements to appropriate Azure AI services
- Choose between Computer Vision, NLP, Machine Learning, and Generative AI
- Design multi-service architectures that integrate Azure AI capabilities
- Apply responsible AI principles across all scenarios
- Make architectural decisions (pre-built vs. custom, real-time vs. batch)

## Challenge

| Challenge | Topic | Focus |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| 24 | End-to-End: Azure AI Portfolio Challenge | All domains — scenario-based decision making |

This challenge simulates exam-style scenario questions where you must select the right service and approach for a given business problem.
