## How to interpret your results

### Mostly ✅: You're over-prepared!
You likely already know much of the AZ-900 content. Consider jumping to [AZ-104](/docs/az-104/overview) instead, or use AZ-900 as a quick confidence-building certification.

### Mix of ✅ and ⚠️: Perfect candidate
AZ-900 is designed exactly for you. Work through the challenges in order and you'll be ready in 1-2 weeks.

### Several ❌: You're the target audience!
Don't worry — AZ-900 assumes NO prior knowledge. The challenges will teach you everything from scratch. Start with [Challenge 01](/docs/az-900/cloud-concepts/challenge-01) and take your time.

:::tip Study Timeline
Most people pass AZ-900 with **1-2 weeks** of focused study. The exam is 45 minutes with ~40-60 questions. If you complete all 24 challenges and their Knowledge Checks, you'll be well-prepared.
:::
