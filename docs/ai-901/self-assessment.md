## How to interpret your results

### Mostly ✅: You're over-prepared!

You likely already know enough to pass. Use the challenges to fill any small gaps and confirm your knowledge. Consider jumping straight to the [exam tips](/docs/ai-901/exam-tips) and taking a practice test.

### Mix of ✅ and ⚠️: Perfect candidate

You have a solid foundation and just need to reinforce specific areas. Work through the challenges in order, focusing extra time on topics marked ⚠️. You'll be exam-ready in no time.

### Several ❌: You're the target audience!

This is exactly who the AI-901 is designed for — people curious about AI who want a structured introduction. Start with [Challenge 01: AI Workloads Overview](/docs/ai-901/ai-concepts/challenge-01) and work through each module sequentially. The challenges build on each other and assume no prior knowledge.
